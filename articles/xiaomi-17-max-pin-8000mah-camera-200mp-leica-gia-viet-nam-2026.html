Xiaomi 17 Max vua ra mat: Pin 8.000mAh, Camera Leica 200MP, Gia Xach Tay Viet Nam Du Kien La Bao Nhieu?

Xiaomi vua trinh lang chiec dien thoai co pin lon nhat trong lich su thuong mai cua hang, va lan dau tien camera Leica 200MP xuat hien ngoai dong Ultra. Dieu bat ngo nhat: gia chi bang mot nua Xiaomi 17 Ultra.

Xiaomi 17 Max chinh thuc ra mat tai Trung Quoc ngay 21/05/2026 — Anh: Gizmochina

Tom tat nhanh

Xiaomi 17 Max ra mat 21/05/2026 tai Trung Quoc, ban ra 25/05/2026. Chip Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, man hinh 6.9 inch OLED 2K 120Hz, do sang dinh 3.500 nits. Camera: 200MP Leica + 50MP periscope 3x + 50MP ultrawide, file anh goc 114MB/tam. Pin 8.000mAh sac nhanh 100W co day + 50W khong day. Gia Trung Quoc tu 4.299 nhan dan te (khoang 15 trieu VND), xach tay Viet Nam du kien 17-19 trieu dong. Chua co ke hoach ban chinh hang Viet Nam.

Xiaomi 17 Max la gi va tai sao no quan trong voi nguoi Viet?

Xiaomi 17 Max la chiec dien thoai lon nhat, nhieu pin nhat trong dong Xiaomi 17 series ra mat nam 2026, ra mat chinh thuc tai Trung Quoc ngay 21/05/2026. Day khong phai dong Ultra cao cap hay Pro Max hai man hinh, ma la mot san pham rieng biet nham vao mot nhu cau rat cu the: nguoi can pin dai, camera tot, chip manh nhung khong muon tra 30-40 trieu dong cho dong Ultra.

Ly do can quan tam den chiec may nay la gia. Trong khi Apple dang chu tam vao iPhone gap nep gia cao ngat nguong, Xiaomi dua ra mot flagship thuc su voi gia chi bang mot nua hoac mot phan ba cac san pham duoc coi la "dang tam" trong phan khuc. Xach tay ve Viet Nam, gia du kien chi tu 17-19 trieu dong, la muc gia cua mot chiec iPhone 17 ban thuong hoac Samsung Galaxy S26 FE, nhung cau hinh thi khac xa.

Theo thong tin tu Gizmochina, san pham bat dau ban ra tai Trung Quoc tu ngay 25/05/2026. Xach tay thuong ve Viet Nam trong vong 1-2 tuan sau do.

Camera 200MP Leica: Lan dau xuat hien ngoai dong Ultra

Day la diem dac biet nhat cua Xiaomi 17 Max. Truoc day, camera co cong nghe Leica Summilux chi xuat hien tren dong Ultra (Xiaomi 15 Ultra, 17 Ultra) voi gia tu 35-40 trieu dong. Lan nay, Xiaomi dua cong nghe do xuong mot chiec may co gia chi bang 40-45% so voi Ultra.

Cu the, Xiaomi 17 Max dung cam bien Samsung HP9 200MP kich thuoc 1/1,4 inch, kem theo xu ly anh Leica. Dieu nay co nghia la: mau sac giu duoc tinh tu nhien kieu Leica, vung ham toi-sang duoc xu ly theo thuong hieu chu khong phai AI "lam dep" qua da. Ngoai ra con co ong kinh periscope 50MP zoom quang hoc 3x (zoom khong mat chat luong den 6x) va ong ultrawide 50MP.

Mot anh toan do 200MP nang 114MB. Theo thu nghiem tu Android Headlines, moi file anh goc o che do 200MP nang den 114,97MB. Voi bo nho 256GB, nguoi dung luu duoc khoang 2.200 tam anh toan do truoc khi day bo. Thuc te, phan lon nguoi dung se chup o che do 50MP hoac 12MP de tiet kiem dung luong.

De so sanh, camera chinh cua iPhone 17 Pro Max la 48MP, Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra la 200MP nhung khong co cong nghe Leica va gia cao gap doi. Xiaomi 17 Max dua ra cam giac "chup anh chuyen nghiep" vao muc gia thuc te hon nhieu.

Pin 8.000mAh: Con so lon nhat trong lich su dien thoai thuong mai Xiaomi

Xiaomi 17 Max dung cong nghe pin silicon-carbon the he moi tu Jinshajiang, voi ham luong silicon dat 16%. Ket qua la cell pin 8.000mAh co the doi trong hon va day hon so voi pin lithium truyen thong cung dung luong, du ket qua giam sat nhiet do tot hon trong qua trinh sac.

De de hinh dung: iPhone 17 Pro Max chi co pin 4.685mAh. Nghia la Xiaomi 17 Max co pin gan gap doi, trong khi gia xach tay chi bang 65% so voi iPhone 17 Pro Max chinh hang. Voi muc su dung thong thuong (mang xa hoi, xem video, chup anh), may du suc di 2 ngay lien tuc ma khong can sac.

Sac nhanh 100W co day va 50W khong day la diem cong lon. Voi 100W, may co the sac tu 0 len 50% trong khoang 15-20 phut. Tinh nang sac khong day 50W cung la dieu it hang nao co o phan khuc gia nay, thong thuong chi xuat hien tren cac may cao cap nhat.

Thang 5/2026 chung kien nhieu ra mat smartphone dang chu y, nhung nhung smartphone tieu bieu nhat thang 5/2026 deu tap trung o phan khuc tren 20 trieu. Xiaomi 17 Max la ngoai le dang gia trong tam gia 17-19 trieu.

Gia xach tay du kien va nen chon phien ban nao cho nguoi dung Viet?

Xiaomi 17 Max chua co lich ban chinh hang tai Viet Nam. Tuy nhien, theo xu huong truoc day, cac may Xiaomi "Max" thuong chi den Viet Nam qua kenh xach tay. May bat dau ban tai Trung Quoc tu 25/05/2026, xach tay du kien ve sau 1-2 tuan, tuc khoang dau thang 6/2026.

Luu y quan trong khi mua xach tay: May dung bang tan Trung Quoc, ho tro 5G SA/NSA bang tan CN. Tai Viet Nam, may van dung duoc 4G/LTE va Wifi 7 binh thuong, nhung mot so bang tan 5G co the khong tuong thich hoan toan voi mang Viettel hoac Mobifone. Can kiem tra truoc khi mua neu co nhu cau dung 5G.

Nen chon phien ban nao? Doi voi phan lon nguoi dung tai Viet Nam, ban 12GB + 256GB du kien 17-18 trieu la lua chon hop ly nhat. Ly do: 256GB la dung luong dang ke, du chua cac anh toan do (neu chup o 50MP thay vi 200MP), video 4K va ung dung thuong ngay. Ban 12GB RAM tu cung du manh de chay da nhiem. Neu hay lam viec voi video hoac luu tru nhieu, ban 16GB + 512GB (~21-22 trieu) dang nang cap len.

De so sanh thuc te: voi cung muc gia 17-19 trieu tai Viet Nam, nguoi mua co the chon iPhone 17 ban thuong (48MP, pin 3.279mAh, chip A18), Samsung Galaxy S26 FE (chip tam trung, pin 4.900mAh), hoac Xiaomi 17 Max voi Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, camera 200MP Leica va pin 8.000mAh. Ve thong so thuan tuy, Xiaomi 17 Max la "re nhat" trong ba lua chon nay khi tinh tren moi dong.

Ket luan: Dang mua hay nen cho chinh hang?

Ket luan ngan: Xiaomi 17 Max la chiec dien thoai dang chu y nhat trong thang 5/2026 cho nguoi Viet co ngan sach 17-22 trieu dong, dac biet nhung ai uu tien pin dai ngay va muon trai nghiem camera Leica ma khong can bo ra 35-40 trieu cho dong Ultra.

Diem manh ro rang: pin lon nhat trong phan khuc gia, camera 200MP Leica lan dau xuat hien o muc gia nay, chip flagship thuc su, sac khong day 50W it chiec may nao co o tam gia tuong duong. Day la cau hinh ma truoc nam 2026, phai tra gap 2-3 lan moi co duoc.

Diem can chuong bi: may chi ban chinh thuc tai Trung Quoc, chua co chinh hang Viet Nam. Xach tay thuong di kem bao hanh shop 6-12 thang thay vi bao hanh hang. Bang tan 5G Trung Quoc co the khong tuong thich hoan toan voi mang VN. Va neu anh goc 200MP nang 114MB, can quan ly bo nho ca than.

Voi nguoi dung quan tam den giao dien phan mem thuan tuy va he sinh thai day du, Android 17 voi Gemini Intelligence tich hop sau ma Xiaomi MIUI chay tren nen tang do van la mot diem cong lon trong trai nghiem hang ngay.

Neu khong can chinh hang va co the chap nhan xach tay, Xiaomi 17 Max la "deal" thuc su o thang 6/2026.

Xiaomi 17 Max gia bao nhieu tai Viet Nam?

Xiaomi 17 Max khac gi so voi Xiaomi 17 Ultra?
Xiaomi 17 Ultra la dong cao cap nhat cua Xiaomi voi gia khoang 40 trieu dong tai Viet Nam, co camera 200MP va pin 6.000mAh nhung kem pin hon. Xiaomi 17 Max co gia re hon nhieu (khoang 17-19 trieu xach tay) nhung lai co pin lon hon han voi 8.000mAh, ho tro sac khong day 50W ma Ultra khong co o muc gia nay.
